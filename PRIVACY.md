# Privacy policy: Insight Extractor helper

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Insight Extractor helper ("the extension") is made by Jaskaran Sethy. This policy covers what it does with data.

## What the extension accesses

When you press **Analyse** on [Insight Extractor](https://jaskaransethy.com/games/insight-extractor) with an Amazon or
Flipkart product link, the extension fetches that product's public review pages from amazon.in, amazon.com or
flipkart.com, from your browser. It passes the pages' HTML to the Insight Extractor page in the same browser tab, which
reads the reviews out of it and analyses them on your device.

Requests to Amazon go out the same way as when you visit Amazon yourself, so if you're signed in to Amazon your
browser's Amazon session is used for those requests. The extension never reads, stores or sends your cookies, login
details or account information.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
- It does not send anything to the developer or to any server other than the shop page being fetched.
- It does not track your browsing, read other websites, or run on any site except jaskaransethy.com.
- It has no analytics and no remote code.

## Data retention

None. The fetched pages exist only in the open Insight Extractor tab and are gone when you close or reload it.
